    Method

    Health Age follows Lee, Esmaeilpour et al. 2026 (Google Research, arXiv:2611.08568): each wearable domain maps to a published hazard ratio, weighted for overlap, and converted to years at 10% extra risk per year of age. The four core domains are resting heart rate, steps, main sleep and Active Zone Minutes; sleep timing, insulin resistance and VO2 max are optional.

    The paper projects futures with SensorWM, a model trained on 96,957 people that is not public. Here a transparent one-person model stands in: steps and sleep follow the plan, Active Zone Minutes follow steps and cardio, and resting heart rate drifts toward a level up to 9% lower with 150 cardio-zone minutes a week (Reimers et al. 2018). Plans rank by projected years divided by one plus effort, where effort is measured against your own week-to-week range.

    Model projections, not medical advice. Independent implementation, not affiliated with Google.

    Connect Google Health

    1. 01

      Make your own Google OAuth client (one time, about 5 minutes). In Google Cloud Console: create a project, enable the Google Health API, set the OAuth consent screen to External and Testing with your Google account as a test user, then create an OAuth client of type Desktop app and download its JSON. Full walkthrough in the README.

    2. 02

      Paste that JSON or load the file. It stays in ~/.healthage.

    3. 03

      Sign in with Google and allow read access. You come back here and the first sync starts.
